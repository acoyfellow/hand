Logo, icons, colour and type.

Mark

An open ring with a dot at the gap. The ring is the screen; the dot is the one place hand chose to press.

Sizes

Keep clear space around the icon equal to a quarter of its width. Below 24 px use favicon.svg.

Icons

One for each step hand takes. Fine lines, square-on, drawn like a part diagram: what the step touches is solid, what it ignores is faint, and blue marks the one thing it acts on.
